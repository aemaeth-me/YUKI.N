## Memory (past chats) setting

- Gemini app "Memories" setting (renamed from "past chats" feature, early 2026): Gemini learns from your past conversations over time — remembers key details and preferences you've shared.
- On by default; can be toggled off anytime. Settings > Personal context > Memory.
- Requirements: 18+, personal Google account (not work/school/supervised), Keep Activity on.
- Not available in certain features (Gems, Live chats). In text chat can reference a past Live conversation.
- Delete chats → Gemini stops using them to personalize (short delay). Ask Gemini "Did you use any info from past chats?" to check.
- Rollout: paid subscribers originally (2025), then expanded to all users in Canada (Feb 26, 2026), UK (Apr 2026), across Gemini 3 models.

## "Ask Gemini to remember" (Saved Info)

- Users can ask Gemini to remember interests and preferences (work, hobbies, aspirations) — "a user manual designed by you."
- Manage info via natural conversation or the dedicated "Saved Info" page — view, edit, delete.
- Gemini displays when it makes use of your saved info.
- Rolling out to more users and 40+ languages (2026); previously English-only for Gemini Advanced (Google One AI Premium).

## Memory / chat history import (switching tools, March 2026)

- **Memory import**: bring key preferences, relationships, personal context from other AI apps. Suggested prompt → paste into your current AI app → copy generated summary back into Gemini → Gemini analyzes and securely saves to Gemini context.
- **Chat history import**: upload a ZIP file of chat history from other AI providers; search past threads and continue building inside Gemini.
- Rolling out to Settings page for all consumer accounts.

## Personal Intelligence (Jan 14, 2026, beta, US)

- Connect Google apps (Gmail, Photos, YouTube, Search) in a single tap to personalize Gemini.
- Gemini accesses your data to answer requests; data already lives at Google — no need to send sensitive data elsewhere.
- Privacy: connecting apps off by default; you choose which apps; can turn off anytime. Correct on the spot ("Remember, I prefer window seats"); regenerate responses without personalization; temporary chats without personalization.
- Gemini references/explains the information used from connected sources so you can verify.
- Initial availability: Google AI Pro and AI Ultra subscribers in US; coming to AI Mode in Search. Personal accounts only.
